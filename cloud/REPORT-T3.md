## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 The source repository Cadre-Sandbox-OpenBridge gained library work after `d9f8655` (to `9727312`) that the owner's migration
workflow calls (NOTE-WFB-10 and NOTE-WFB-12; register items COB-207 to COB-212). This tranche brings that work into this
repository so that it becomes a superset of both lines and, under ruling R7, the only line of the library.

1.2 Out of scope by the rulings: the source's wrap of the generator on `obc-automation-button` (R1), any edit of the element map
(R2), any module build, version change or rebuild of the installed module (R5) and any change of the pinned element release (R6).

## 2. Method

2.1 **The patch.** The 42 files the patch modifies have, at `26f38c8`, exactly the preimage blobs the patch names (the abbreviated
hashes of `d9f8655`); the 32 files it adds are absent there. On `sandbox-delta` at `26f38c8`, `git apply --index` applied the
patch with no rejects (one whitespace warning: a blank last line of a new file, kept as the source has it). Commit `0a35cbc`
carries the source's commit list in its message.

2.2 **The merge.** `port/sandbox-0.8.0` was created from `import/sandbox-9727312` and `sandbox-delta` merged into it with
`git merge --no-ff` (base `26f38c8`). 33 files had changed on both sides (12 under `tools/hmi`, `tools/compare/glass_gates.py`,
`library/profile.json`, `schemas/screen-descriptor.schema.json`, 10 under `module/`, 5 tests and 3 wiki pages; `NOTICE` had
changed on the source side only). Git named 14 of them as conflicted, with 35 hunks. Each hunk was resolved by reading both sides
and the contract (section 3). The merge commit is `11005e3`. Its message and the first progress line say 15 conflicted files;
the count is 14, as listed in Table 1.

2.3 **Generated files.** `library/contracts.json`, `library/profile.json` and `build/cadre_hmi_base` were regenerated by
`tools/hmi/parent.py`; the four tracked child builds by `screen.py` and `showcase.py`; `cadre-ob.js`, the schemas and
`module/components.json` by `npm run build` and `module/tools/gen_module_assets.py`, which wrote them byte-identical. The elements
list and the pin manifest came from the patch and were checked (section 6, items 2 and 3).

2.4 **Delegation.** Four bounded tasks ran in subagents on Sonnet 5.5 at high effort: the map proposal (R2), the generator-wrap
tests (R1 and R4), wiki pages 16 to 18, and the review of section 11. Their results were checked in the main session before
commit: each changed test was read against the R4 rule, the wiki pages against the code, and the proposal's basis against the
committed tree.

2.5 **Commits on the branch**, in order: `0a35cbc` (the patch), `11005e3` (the merge with its resolutions and regenerated files),
`0a442c1` (tests ported under R4), `7a9e402` (GF-117: the local assembly's views), `1daa6fe` (the product name out of new prose),
`0d25aa1` (documentation), then the records and the reviewer's corrections (section 11).
