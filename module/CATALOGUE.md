## 8. Elements Not Wrapped

1. The nine OpenBridge line elements (`obc-bus`, `obc-corner-line`, `obc-direction-line`, `obc-end-point-line`, `obc-horizontal-line`, `obc-vertical-line`, `obc-three-way-line`, `obc-line-cross`, `obc-line-overlap`) are skipped by the owner's decision of tranche T1: they overlap the library's pipes and the flow solver (gate G6).
2. `obc-automation-input-modal` has four bare slots and no attribute; a property cannot fill a slot, so it needs a container component (`wiki/12` section 17.20).
3. `obc-alert-list` has rows as slotted children and no data property (`wiki/12` section 17.22.6); the module's alarm lists wrap `obc-alert-list-details` and `obc-alert-list-page-small`.
